# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 25, 2026_

Hubo is an instruction-only plugin. It has no server, accounts, cookies, analytics, telemetry, or database. Hubo itself does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

Hubo runs inside supported AI coding hosts and works with the conversations, files, and tools you authorize there. Those hosts and tools may process data under their own privacy policies. Hubo does not give them additional access or control how they handle data.

Hubo deliberately shows its worker/reviewer exchange in the current conversation so you can review it. That exchange may contain code, file paths, commands, and test output. Hubo does not keep a separate copy or send it to an external Hubo service; conversation storage and retention are controlled by the host.

If a future Hubo release adds data collection or an external service, this policy will be updated before that release.

Questions? [Open an issue](https://github.com/h0ngcha0/hubo/issues).
